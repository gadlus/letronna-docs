Tap the model's name at the top of a chat to open the **Model** menu.

The menu lists Letronna's [hosted models](/docs/hosted-models) with their prices, per 1M tokens in and out. Models that cost nothing say **Free**. A model with effort levels lets you choose its effort.

## Finding a model

**Search models** finds one by name. You can sort and filter the list:

| Sort | Shows first |
|---|---|
| **Suggested** | the models Letronna suggests |
| **Cheapest** | the lowest price |
| **Longest context** | the most text a model can read at once |
| **A–Z** | by name |

| Filter | Keeps |
|---|---|
| **Reasoning** | models that think before they answer |
| **Reads images** | models that can read photos |
| **1M context** | models that read 1M tokens or more |

## Models on the iPhone

On an iPhone, the menu also has a group **On this iPhone**: Apple Intelligence, and the models you have downloaded. They answer with no internet. **Manage** opens the list of them. See [Working offline](/docs/mobile/offline).

## Chats on your computer

A chat that runs on your computer uses your computer's models, not this menu. Its name shows where the model's name would. See [Your computer](/docs/mobile/your-computer).
