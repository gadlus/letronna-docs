## Starting a chat

Type in **Ask Letronna anything** and tap **Send**. Tap **Stop** to stop an answer. The first message names the chat.

**New chat** is at the top of the screen and in the chats list.

An answer keeps going when you move to another chat. The chats list shows a moving mark beside a chat that is still answering. If an answer was cut off while the app was closed, it is asked again when you come back.

## The chats list

Swipe to the right anywhere on the chat to open the list. Chats are grouped as Pinned, Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier. **Search** finds a chat by its name.

Hold a chat in the list for:

| Choice | Does |
|---|---|
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | keeps it at the top |
| **Rename** | gives it your own name |
| **Name it again** | lets Letronna name it again from what was said |
| **Delete** | deletes it; **Undo** brings it back for a moment |

## Holding a message

Hold any message for:

| Choice | Does |
|---|---|
| **Copy** | copies the whole message |
| **Select text** | opens the message so you can pick any part of it |
| **Share** | opens the phone's share sheet |
| **Edit** | edits your own message and asks again |
| **Retry** | asks for that answer again |
| **Retry in a fork** | asks again in a new chat; the original keeps what came after |
| **Fork from here** | copies the chat up to here into a new chat, named "(fork)" |
| **Rewind to here** | removes what came after; **Undo** brings it back |

Each answer shows how many tokens went in and came out.

## Voice input

Tap the microphone in the composer and speak. The first time, allow the microphone and speech recognition.

By default your words are transcribed by ElevenLabs Scribe on Letronna, from your credit. The phone transcribes instead, for free, when Letronna cannot be reached or your account has no credit. To always use the phone, see [Settings](/docs/mobile/settings).

## When an answer stops short

The chat says why. For example:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your credit has run out. Top up on letronna.ai to keep chatting. | add credit on letronna.ai; the app does not sell credit |
| That model is not served any more. Pick another from the menu. | choose another [model](/docs/mobile/models) |
| Too many messages at once. Wait a moment and send again. | wait, then send again |
| No connection. Check your internet and send again. | on an iPhone, see [Working offline](/docs/mobile/offline) |
