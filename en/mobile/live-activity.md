## Live Activity

On an iPhone, an answer being written shows as a Live Activity: in the Dynamic Island, and on the lock screen. It shows the chat's name, how long it has been working, and the last words written. When the answer is in, it says **Ready**; if it stopped, **Stopped**. Tap it to go back to the app.

## Notifications

If you leave the app while Letronna is answering, a notification says "Letronna is working". When the answer is in, another says "Done:" and the chat's name. If it did not finish, one says "Letronna stopped". The app asks to send notifications the first time it has something to tell you.

While you are in the app, the chat shows everything, and no banner appears.

iOS lets an app run only a little while after you leave it. A long answer may wait until you come back; then it carries on.
