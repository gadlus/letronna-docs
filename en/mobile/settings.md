title: "Settings"
description: "What each part of the mobile app's Settings does."
---

Open the chats, then **Settings** at the foot of the list.

| Group | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Account** | your **Credit**, and what you **Spent, 30 days** with the number of asks |
| **Computers** | your computers, and **Pair with a computer**; see [Your computer](/docs/mobile/your-computer) |
| **GitHub** | the link for [code sessions](/docs/mobile/code-sessions) |
| **Signed in on** | the phones and browsers your account is on; see [Signing in](/docs/mobile/signing-in) |
| **Appearance** | **System**, **Night** or **Paper** |
| **Language** | **Phone**, English or 日本語 |
| **Web search** | **Auto**, **Free** or **Paid**; see [Web search and photos](/docs/mobile/search-and-photos) |
| **Voice input** | who transcribes what you say |
| **On this iPhone** | models on the phone; see [Working offline](/docs/mobile/offline) |

Credit is added on letronna.ai, not in the app. Pull down on Settings to load it again.

## Voice input

| Choice | Does |
|---|---|
| **Letronna** | ElevenLabs Scribe on Letronna transcribes it; the price a minute shows here, from your credit |
| **On phone** | the phone transcribes it, for free; less accurate, and only in the app's language |

With **Letronna**, the phone still transcribes when Letronna cannot be reached, or the account has no credit. **Model** picks which transcription model to use.

## Language

The app follows your phone's language unless you pick one. The keyboard and system sheets always follow the phone.
