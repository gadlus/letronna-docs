---
title: "Signing in"
description: "Signing in to the mobile app, and signing out of other phones and browsers."
---

The app uses your letronna.ai account. You need the internet to sign in the first time.

1. Open the app.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, or type your email under **Personal or work email** and choose **Continue with email**.
3. If you used email, type the code sent to you.

If the account was made another way, the app says "This account signs in another way." Use that way instead.

## Where you are signed in

Settings > **Signed in on** lists each phone and browser your account is signed in on, and when it was last used. "This phone" is the one in your hand.

To sign out somewhere else, tap it and choose **Sign out**. That phone or browser has to sign in again.

## Signing out

Settings > **Sign out**, at the foot of Settings.
