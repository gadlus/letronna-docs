---
title: "Working offline"
description: "Chatting with no internet on an iPhone: Apple Intelligence and models you download to the phone."
---

On an iPhone, chat can answer with no internet, from a model that runs on the phone's own chip. What you type and the photos you send stay on the phone. This is on iPhone only.

With no internet, a bar says "You are offline. Chats on this phone still open." If an answer cannot reach Letronna, it is written on the phone instead, and says which model answered.

## The models

Open Settings > **On this iPhone** > **Models**, or **Manage** in the model menu.

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| Apple Intelligence | built into iOS, no download; turn it on in the iPhone's Settings |
| Ternary Bonsai 2 · 4B | download |
| Ternary Bonsai 2 · 2B | download |
| Qwen3.5 2B | download |
| Qwen3.5 4B | download |

The screen shows each model's size and what is free on the phone. Bigger models answer better, but take more space and battery. A model too big for your iPhone's memory says so before it downloads.

## Downloading one

1. Tap **Get** beside a model.
2. Read the size, and tap **Get** again. Wi-Fi is best; the app warns you on mobile data.
3. Keep chatting while it downloads.

Tap a model for more: **Pause download**, **Resume download**, **Cancel download**, **Use in chat**, **Delete from iPhone** and **View on Hugging Face**. To add other models, see [Adding a model](/docs/mobile/add-model).

## Photos

The downloaded models read text at first. To let one read photos, choose **Add photo reading** on it: a second download, a few hundred MB. Photos are read on the phone and stay on it. Apple Intelligence reads text only.

## Web search

With internet, Ternary Bonsai 2 · 4B and Qwen3.5 can search the web through Letronna. Only the search words leave the phone.

## Limits

A chat that grows too long for a model on the phone says "This chat is too long for a model on the phone. Start a new chat." If a model will not load for lack of memory, close some apps or pick a smaller one.
