---
title: "Teams"
description: "One credit pool for 2 to 5 people: seats, spending limits, the audit log, and who pays."
---

A team shares one credit pool: everyone's hosted models draw from it. Make one on the [Team page](/dashboard/team), start the Team plan, and invite people there.

## Seats

- Team is $18 a seat a month ($183.60 a year), from 2 to 5 seats, and each seat adds its value to the pool.
- Every member uses a seat; add a seat before inviting someone past them. A seat added mid-month is charged in full, and the pool gets the same amount.
- An admin can remove a seat from the next renewal: the seats paid for stay until then, and nothing is refunded.

## Admins

- Set a monthly spending limit per member; they stop at it until the next month or a higher limit.
- Add credit to the pool any time, from $1.
- The audit log lists every change: who joined or left, roles, limits, seats, the plan and credit.
- Someone removed from the team stops using its pool at once.

## Who pays

Each computer picks whether hosted models are paid by you or by a team you are in: `/team` in the terminal, or Settings > Account in the app.
