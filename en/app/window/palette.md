---
title: "The command palette"
description: "One box to run an action, open a page, find a session, pick a model or attach a folder."
---

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on macOS), or click **Search or run a command** in the top bar. Type an action, a page, a session, a model or a path.

Up and Down move, Enter goes, and Escape closes it.

## What it finds

| Group | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Actions** | things to do now, such as New session, Show or hide the panel, Show the keys, Go back to an earlier message, Switch Crew on or off, and the three appearances |
| **Go to** | the window's pages, such as Capabilities, Messaging and Activity, and each Settings page, marked Settings |
| **Sessions** | your sessions, newest first; a new session shows once something is said in it |
| **Skills** | your skills, to use one |
| **Models** | every model you can pick, with its gateway, to switch to it |
| **Folders** | folders your sessions used lately, to attach one to this session |

**Choose a project folder** browses for a folder to attach. Signed out, **Sign in** is offered under **Letronna account**.

A row shows its key, where it has one. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/app/window/shortcuts).
