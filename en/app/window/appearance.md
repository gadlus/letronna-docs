---
title: "Appearance"
description: "The window's themes, film grain and motion."
---

Open Settings > Appearance.

## Theme

| Theme | Looks |
|---|---|
| **Night** | midnight blue around a dark ink page; the default |
| **Paper** | ink on a light page, for daylight |
| **Follow system** | Night or Paper, as the computer is set, switching when it does |

The [command palette](/docs/app/window/palette) switches them too: type **Night appearance**, **Paper appearance** or **Follow the system's appearance**.

## Film grain

**Film grain** lays a faint grain on the frame and the empty pages, as letronna.ai has. It is never drawn over the conversation, code or diffs.

## Motion

With **Motion** on, dialogs, panels and new messages ease in. It is always off while your computer is set to reduce motion.
