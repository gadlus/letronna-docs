Both need you signed in to a Letronna account, and both are paid from your credit. See [Hosted models and credit](/docs/hosted-models).

## Speaking a message

1. Click the microphone beside the composer, or press Ctrl+Shift+V (Cmd+Shift+V on macOS).
2. Speak. The button turns red and counts the time.
3. Click it again, or press the same keys, to stop.

It shows **Transcribing…**, then types what you said at the cursor. Nothing is sent until you send it. If it heard nothing, it says **No speech was heard.**

A recording stops by itself after two minutes. The sound is kept only while it is turned into text, then deleted.

Signed out, the button says **Sign in to use voice input**.

## Replies read aloud

Switch on **Read replies aloud** in Settings > Thinking. Each reply is then spoken as it streams, and a voice note sent from a [chat app](/docs/chat-apps) is answered with one. It is paid by the character.

In the terminal, `/voice` does the same.
