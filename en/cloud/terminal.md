title: "A terminal in the browser"
description: "Open Letronna itself, running in Letronna Cloud, in a terminal on the Cloud page."
---

The [Cloud page](/dashboard/cloud) can open Letronna itself, running in Letronna Cloud, in a terminal on the page. It is the same agent as [in your terminal](/docs/terminal), and you type into it the same way.

1. On the Cloud page, under Terminal, pick a Size: Light, Normal or Heavy (see [Tasks](/docs/cloud/tasks) for what each holds).
2. Choose Open a terminal, for a new thread. To carry on a thread you have, open one from that thread instead.

## How long it stays open

It is closed:

- after 15 minutes without a key,
- after 2 minutes with no page showing it,
- after 2 hours in all,
- or once your credit runs out.

Its thread's folder and session stay, so opening it again carries on. You may have two open at once, and each counts as one of your tasks.

## What it costs

It is billed by the second while it is open, as a task is, with the model tokens it uses on top. See [Costs](/docs/cloud/costs).
