---
title: "What Letronna Cloud costs"
description: "How tasks, services and web apps are charged, and where to see what you spent."
---

Letronna Cloud comes with [Pro](/dashboard/billing) and is paid from your credit. There are two kinds of charge: the compute, and the models a task uses.

## Compute

Compute is about **$6 per GB a month**, charged by the second for the memory in use.

| What | Charged |
|---|---|
| A task | by the second it runs, for its size's memory |
| A terminal in the browser | by the second it is open |
| A service | by the hour, while it runs |
| A web app | by the second, only while it is awake |
| A live link to what a task made | nothing |

Examples, online all month:

| Service | About |
|---|---|
| 128 MB | $0.75 |
| 256 MB | $1.50 |

A stopped service, and a sleeping web app, cost nothing.

## Models

The models a task uses are paid as any hosted request, at the prices on the [models page](/dashboard/models), with Pro's lower prices. See [Hosted models and credit](/docs/hosted-models).

## When credit runs out

A service is stopped and a terminal is closed. [Add credit](/dashboard/billing) and start them again.

## Seeing what you spent

- The [Cloud page](/dashboard/cloud) shows what each service and web app has cost, how long it was billed, and each task with its compute.
- The [usage page](/dashboard/usage) lists every Cloud charge, day by day, beside text and media.
