The [dashboard](/dashboard) is your account on letronna.ai. Sign in there to see your credit, your computers and what you have spent. Its menu has three parts.

## Workspace

| Page | Shows |
|---|---|
| [Overview](/dashboard) | your last thirty days: credit, what you spent, requests, your plan, the models you used most and your signed-in computers |
| [Usage](/dashboard/usage) | everything billed over thirty days: text, media and Letronna Cloud, day by day and by model, with each model's published price beside what you paid |
| [Cloud](/dashboard/cloud) | what you host on [Letronna Cloud](/docs/cloud), with its output, Stop, Start and Restart, and the runs you sent |
| [Models](/dashboard/models) | every hosted model, with its price, its price on Pro, its context and output limits, and what it can do |

On Overview, **On us today** lists models Letronna pays for, up to a limit each day. The limit starts again at 00:00 UTC. On the Free plan with no credit, only those models answer.

## Account

| Page | Does |
|---|---|
| [Billing](/dashboard/billing) | your plan and credit; Add credit, Manage subscription, and the history of payments with their receipts |
| [Team](/dashboard/team) | make a team and manage it; see [Teams](/docs/teams) |
| [Referrals](/dashboard/referrals) | your invite link; see below |
| [$LETRO](/dashboard/letro) | your LETRO wallet; see [LETRO](/docs/letro) |
| [Devices](/dashboard/devices) | the computers signed in to your account, each with when it was added and last used, and Sign out |
| [Settings](/dashboard/settings) | your account: your profile and how you sign in |

Signing a computer out on Devices signs out Letronna on it. To add a computer, install Letronna on it and sign in when it asks.

## Install

[Download](/dashboard/download) offers Letronna for your system first. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).

## Referrals

Share your invite link. When someone signs up with it and first pays for Pro or Team, you each get credit on your account. The page shows the reward, how many signed up and subscribed, and what you have earned. There is no limit on how many people you invite.
