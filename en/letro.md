LETRO is Letronna's token on Robinhood Chain. Buying credit with it and staking it switch on when LETRO launches; until then the [LETRO page](/dashboard/letro) shows how they will work.

- **Your wallet**: made for your account on request, with no seed phrase and nothing to install. Send LETRO to it from MetaMask, any wallet or an exchange, and keep a little ETH there for network fees.
- **Credit**: $10 worth of LETRO becomes $10 of credit at the day's rate. Of the LETRO paid, 10% is burned for good and 90% goes to the Letronna treasury, which runs the service.
- **Staking**: lock LETRO for 90 days (it renews unless you turn that off) and get 20% a year of its value as AI credit, every day, spent before your credit, up to $5 a day. Leave early any time: 90% back, 10% burned.
- **Burns** go to the dead address, where nobody can move them, each one a transaction you can check on the explorer.
