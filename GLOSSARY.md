# Glossary

The words each language uses for Letronna's terms, so every page says the same thing the same way. Use these when you translate. To change a term, open an issue: it has to change on every page of that language at once, and on letronna.ai.

Product names never change: Letronna, LETRO, Letronna Cloud, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama, GitHub, and the names of plans and models.

## Mistakes to avoid

Native reviewers found these in earlier translations. Check your page for them:

- **A pronoun the language drops**: あなた and 당신 (romantic in "당신 없이도"), six or seven times each. Say whose it is only where it matters (お使いの, 내, 본인의).
- **A headline split into fragments with a full stop** ("전에.", "하는 일."): Korean headlines have none; split where the phrase breaks.
- **A word with a stronger everyday meaning**: 裁判 (a referee), 席 and 좌석 (a seat in a hall), 심판, حَكَم, メモリ (RAM), 감시기 (a surveillance device), 지원 (also: applying), التفويض (authorization).
- **No subject, no object**: "It keeps working" attached to the laptop (它), "ask in the chat" had no object. Name the agent, the task, the window (an app window).
- **A bare "key"** is a height, a cryptographic key or a keyboard key: API key.
- **One idea, four verbs**: Korean said billed, paid, charged and deducted for the same thing. Credit is deducted.
- **Latin names with a prefix glued on** in Arabic (وLinux): repeat the noun (ومقابل Letronna Cloud) or use commas; no tatweel before a Latin word.
- **The count of a thing that must be said** ("提高三倍" can mean four times; "256 MB" of what?).


## Tone per language

- **German**: du in marketing, app and dashboard; Sie on the legal pages (counsel decides). „Anführungszeichen“. Compounds with English words take a hyphen (Web-App, Audit-Log).
  The cost is the subject of "abgebucht", never the service. "Seat" stays English.
- **Spanish**: neutral Spanish, tú in the product, usted or impersonal in legal text (decided with counsel); no vosotros. «Comillas», sentence case. Laptop, portátil or ordenador, and
  alojados or hospedados, is a decision made once for the whole site.
- **Hebrew**: no niqqud; quotation marks are "…" (the gershayim ״ is for acronyms, not quotes). The reader is addressed in the gender-neutral plural (שלכם, ששילמתם); only a command the user
  speaks to the agent is masculine singular. Latin words take a hyphenated prefix (ב-Rust, ל-Letronna Cloud); plan names stay Latin; no comma before the conjunction in a list. A command the user speaks to the agent is a true imperative (עקוב, שלח, השאר), not the colloquial future (תשאיר); the agent's reply does not say "your" (שלכם) where the user wrote in the singular.
